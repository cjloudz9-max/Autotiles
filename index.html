Visual sync: if tiles reach the line before you hear the beat, press +.

Tap when a tile glows and says TAP. Long tiles: press and hold until they end. Tapping a little early is fine. Anywhere in the lane works (or press D F J K). Amber SWIPE tiles: swipe sideways the way the arrow points (or press ← / →). Tiles speed up when the song speeds up.
